Placing the phone

  • Stand the phone up on the counter on the student's side of the monitor (or on top of it), bottom edge pointing at them. Every halving of the distance is like them speaking twice as loud.
  • Don't put it behind the monitor or flat behind your keyboard — the screen blocks the voice.
  • Watch the green bar under the mic while they talk: it should reach about half. If it barely moves, raise Mic boost; if it turns orange, lower it.
  • Keep the phone plugged in and this page open in Safari (the screen stays on by itself).
  • Say "next student" to clear, "stop" / "wait" to cancel an auto-add, "go ahead" to add now.
