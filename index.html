Mic tips

  • Lay the phone flat on the desk between you and the student, bottom edge (the mic) toward them.
  • Numbers are the hardest part. For best accuracy tap the 🎤 next to a field — it listens for just that one thing.
  • Ask them to say the number in chunks: "972… 555… 1234."
  • If speech stops working: Settings → General → Keyboard → Enable Dictation, and Settings → Apps → Safari → Microphone → Allow. The keyboard's own 🎤 also works in any box.
